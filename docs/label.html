NEISS-AI · help check its accuracy

Label injury stories without seeing NEISS-AI's answers

NEISS-AI reads short injury stories written by hospital emergency department staff. Its accuracy has been tested, but the answer keys were not made by outside human experts. You can help close that gap: read each story and choose the answers yourself. You never see what NEISS-AI decided, so your answers are independent. I then publish how well they agree, whatever the result, and credit you by name only if you want.

1. About you

Used only to credit you (if you agree) and to describe the reviewers in the results, for example "an injury epidemiologist at a European university".

2. Choose a set

Each story takes about 15–30 seconds. Label as many as you like (even 50 helps); your progress is saved in this browser, so you can stop and come back.

The stories are public US CPSC NEISS data. They describe injuries in plain clinical language.
